How to use it

  1. On iPhone (Safari): tap View in AR. Point the camera at the target and the PNG snaps onto it.
  2. On a Mac: download the .usdz and AirDrop it to your iPhone. It opens straight in AR Quick Look.
  3. Once it's open, tap the AR tab at the top, then aim at the target image. Nothing shows until the image is found.

Best targets: sharp, high-contrast images with lots of detail and no big flat areas or repeating patterns. Matte prints track better than glossy ones. The example loaded here is generated on the page, so you can try it by pointing your phone at your screen (set the width to what you see on screen).
